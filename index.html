Privacy

Less, by design.

This site has no forms, advertising trackers, third-party analytics, embedded widgets or cookies.

I keep simple, self-hosted statistics to see how the site is read: which sections are viewed, which links are clicked, how far a page is scrolled, how long it is read, the referring site, and the approximate city, network provider, device, browser, screen size and language.

IP addresses are never stored. They are used only to make an anonymous identifier that changes every day and cannot be reversed. There is no fingerprinting, no attempt to identify you, no sharing, and data is deleted after 90 days. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is counted.

Your language and appearance choices stay in your own browser. External links follow their own privacy practices.
