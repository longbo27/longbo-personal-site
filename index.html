PROJECT NOTES / PERSONAL PROJECT

Longbo Photography

A photograph-first publishing platform. The goal is not simply to display images, but to bring content management, image processing, and publication together as a coherent product.

Product meets implementation

The project uses Next.js, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL, with a separate image-processing worker. My focus spans the product concept, system structure, interface details, and the process of building and releasing it.

An independent home for the work

The photography platform has its own site and infrastructure. This personal homepage does not load or share its backend, database, image storage, or administration systems.

THE APPROACH / INDEPENDENT PRACTICE

Start with the purpose.

A good website starts with a clear purpose: who it is for, what it needs to communicate, and what it helps someone do.

Think → design → build → refine

I enjoy connecting these stages rather than treating them as separate hand-offs. Structure, content, interface, implementation, and ongoing maintenance should serve the same goal.

This personal website follows that same philosophy: a static, considered experience with clear content, thoughtful details, and no unnecessary complexity.

This is a description of personal practice and approach, not a client case study, a promise of commercial results, or an institutional endorsement.

A LIGHTER DIGITAL FOOTPRINT

Less collection. More intention.

This website has no contact forms, advertising trackers, third-party analytics scripts, or embedded social widgets. It does not ask you to submit personal information.

When you choose a language or motion setting, the site may save that display preference in your browser. Those preferences are not sent to me.

As with other websites, the hosting provider may process routine connection data, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the site. LinkedIn, GitHub, and the separate photography site have their own privacy practices when you follow those links.

This homepage operates independently of the photography platform, without shared logins, databases, or analytics.
